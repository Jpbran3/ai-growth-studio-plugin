### 1. Executive Summary

In plain language: what the evidence supports, the most important gap, what to do first, and what remains unproven. Financial headline only when supported, always potential and conditional. No agency branding.

### 2. Current Lead-to-Job Funnel

Use only relevant stages. Table: Stage | Count/range | Period/cohort/definition | Source status | Progression or gap. Mark unknowns and pending leads. Show a simple arrow journey if useful. Distinguish accepted from completed/paid jobs.

### 3. Revenue Leak Map

Table: Leak/stage | Evidence | Likely cause and cause status | Gap confidence | Priority and reason. Suspected leaks stay suspected. Retention uses a separate relevant cohort.

### 4. Top Opportunities

For each material opportunity, show input labels, formula, conservative/base/high if justified, potential jobs/value, projection confidence, capacity/overlap limitations. If dollars cannot be supported, describe operational impact and exactly what data would permit quantification. Never substitute a made-up estimate to fill a section.

### 5. What Should Be Fixed

Tie each intervention to cause/evidence. Explain why process, routing, staffing or software fits, and what observation could change that choice.

### 6. Quick Wins

Small actions the owner can take with current resources, suggested role, practical check and relative window. Do not invent cost savings.

### 7. Automation Opportunities

Only warranted uses. For each: task, preconditions, accountable role, exception/human handoff, success metric and simpler alternative. State “not warranted by current evidence” when appropriate. Separate conventional reminders/task automation from AI.

### 8. Larger System Improvements

Dependencies and sustainable ownership, capacity, handoffs and measurement. No unnecessary enterprise stack for a solo operator.

### 9. Important Assumptions / Data Gaps

Evidence ledger summary including inputs, labels, period/cohort, formulas, unresolved conflicts, unknowns, website access limits and next measurement. Confidence in observations is separate from projected recovery.

### 10. Implementation Blueprint

Table: Step / linked finding | Suggested accountable role | Action / dependencies | Relative window | Measure / baseline | Review and exception path. Sequence validation, operational fix, measurement, then automation if justified. Use feasible 0–7 / 8–30 / 31–60 day windows as suggestions, adjusting to the business; never portray them as agreed commitments.
