# Quote-pool triage

A quote record is not necessarily a unique opportunity. Revisions/options belong to one underlying project; distinct real projects from the same customer need not be merged. Define the deduplication key and period before counting. Use anonymized aggregates/IDs; no customer PII is needed in an owner report.

## Sequential, mutually exclusive pool

Estimate records − excess revisions/duplicate records = unique opportunities.
Unique opportunities − draft/undelivered opportunities = delivered quotes.
Delivered quotes − accepted/closed/ineligible opportunities = still-open delivered opportunities.
Still-open delivered opportunities − normal pending/not-due opportunities = suitable unresolved follow-up pool.

Accepted/closed/ineligible group includes accepted jobs, explicit declines/lost work, unsuitable service/area, expired/replaced invalid quotes and do-not-contact prospects; count each opportunity once even if multiple reasons apply. Normal pending/not-due includes customers still within their stated decision period or agreed later check-in. An undelivered draft can be a delivery/production issue, but not a delivered-quote follow-up opportunity. Separate that finding.

Unknown status/exclusions are gaps, never zero. Do not subtract overlapping category totals twice. A directly supported suitable unresolved unique-project count can bypass this waterfall. If uncertain, classify known projects qualitatively or provide clearly assumed coherent sensitivity cases rather than inventing counts.

## Which quotes to prioritize

Compare customer interest/timing, service fit, relevant history, available capacity and practicality of the next action. High quoted value alone is not high priority. State a reason and proposed next step, not a probability score. Buckets:
- Ready for an appropriate next action: delivered, unresolved, suitable, contact preference permits, timing supports contact; assign owner and next step.
- Clarification needed: delivery, scope, objection or status uncertain; validate before selling or projecting money.
- Future decision: record customer-stated due date and preferred channel; do not pressure or label normal waiting as lost.
- Closed/ineligible: keep historical reasons; do not add to recoverable backlog or repeat outreach after a decline/opt-out.

A sample follow-up may ask whether the customer received the quote or needs scope/timing clarified. Match known facts, no fabricated availability/discounts/urgency. Drafting does not imply authorization to send. No universal number of contacts or follow-up days is built in.
