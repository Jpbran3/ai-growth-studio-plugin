# Practical process design

Map constraints and propose feasible options: validated duration estimates, geographic grouping, reserved contingency, skill-aware assignment, owner-reviewed waitlists and accurate customer promises. Explain tradeoffs and downstream dependencies; avoid automatic overtime, layoffs, pricing changes or supplier promises. A proposed schedule uses supplied availability and constraints with explicit tentative labels. No live calendar changes, employee messages, job dispatch or booking commitments occur.

Suggested assignments, timing and targets are proposals until the owner agrees. Give a manual alternative and failure path for every automation recommendation. Confirm inputs, accountable role and exception handling before any later implementation.
