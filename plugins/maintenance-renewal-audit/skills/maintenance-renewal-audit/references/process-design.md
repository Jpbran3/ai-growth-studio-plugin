# Practical process design

Build an owner-reviewed due/renewal register with agreement reference, minimal account/asset key, documented due basis, responsible role, next action, exceptions and completion evidence. Prioritize agreed commitments and deliverability. Separate service scheduling from an optional renewal invitation. Draft factual reminders grounded in actual terms; do not imply automatic enrollment or billing. No live reminders, renewals, charges, calendar edits or account changes. If contract interpretation is requested, obtain current authoritative guidance rather than improvising legal conclusions.

Suggested assignments, timing and targets are proposals until the owner agrees. Give a manual alternative and failure path for every automation recommendation. Confirm inputs, accountable role and exception handling before any later implementation.
