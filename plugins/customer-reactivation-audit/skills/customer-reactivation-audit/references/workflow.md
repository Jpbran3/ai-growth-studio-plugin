# Customer Reactivation Audit: diagnosis

Journey: past completed customer → appropriate current need → contact eligibility → response → booked work → completed repeat job.

Age of a customer record does not establish a current need or permission to contact. Deduplicate households/accounts and separate completed customers from old prospects and quote leads. Determine service relevance, geography, known ownership changes, existing active jobs, recent outreach, unresolved complaints, preferences and opt-outs. Record eligibility as eligible, excluded or unknown with reason; unknown contact preference is not assumed authorization. Do not invent an equipment replacement need or use unsupported scare tactics. Route due recurring obligations to maintenance renewal rather than counting them twice.

Look for appropriate service relevance and the owner's ability to deliver before proposing a campaign. An eligible list can be small or empty. Diagnose why customers stopped returning without assuming neglect: one-time work, moving, satisfaction, price or no current need may explain it.

Separate symptom, cause hypothesis, alternative explanation and validation step. Labels and limitations belong with findings. Use anonymized examples/aggregates where possible; no credentials or unnecessary customer information. A brief focused report is preferable to repeating unrelated funnel questions.
