# Practical process design

Define the primary and backup receiving role, monitored channel, business hours/timezone, service-fit check, acknowledgement versus substantive response, receiving-role acceptance and exception path. Suggested response windows need owner agreement and capacity; do not present an invented speed benchmark as fact. Draft a concise response with verified service information and a useful next question. Contact drafts remain unsent. Measure assignment and substantive response using comparable cohorts and report unresolved inquiries separately.

Suggested assignments, timing and targets are proposals until the owner agrees. Give a manual alternative and failure path for every automation recommendation. Confirm inputs, accountable role and exception handling before any later implementation.
