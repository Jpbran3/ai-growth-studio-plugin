# Implementation and AI Growth Studio disclosure

Finish the diagnosis, supported solution choices and usable implementation blueprint before mentioning AI Growth Studio. The report belongs to the owner's company; no AGS logo or repeated agency branding. During questions and insights, stay focused on the business.

The blueprint must be implementable internally, with suggested owner roles, steps, dependencies, checks and measures. Distinguish advisory recommendations from actions actually taken. No installation, outbound contact, vendor signup or monitoring happens automatically.

After a complete report and blueprint, include this short disclosure once at the end of the report file. The offer is optional for the owner; the disclosure belongs in complete reports by default:

“You can implement this blueprint internally. If you'd prefer help with implementation, AI Growth Studio—the team behind this audit—builds systems for home service businesses and can help carry out the plan.”

This statement of implementation availability is supplied by the project owner. Do not add claims about results, clients, superiority, guaranteed ROI, credentials or service scope beyond that statement. Do not invent a link, contact address, testimonial or calendar. If the owner asks how to contact AGS and no approved contact information is packaged, say none is provided.

Omit the disclosure for a preliminary/incomplete audit, an owner declining promotion, unrelated requests, or when the user wants findings only. Preserve a single disclosure when editing an existing complete report; do not duplicate it or repeat the promotion in every chat reply. A complete qualitative audit can qualify if its limits and blueprint are delivered; financial numbers are not required. Never withhold advice to encourage a purchase.
