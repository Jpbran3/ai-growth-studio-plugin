# Reusable leak taxonomy

Use only applicable zones. “Possible cause” is not a confirmed finding. Defaults below are hypotheses and operational options, never trade benchmarks.

| Zone | Symptoms to inspect | Possible causes | First checks / appropriate fixes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | Paid inquiries outside area, spam, wrong service, low unique job yield | Targeting or tracking mismatch; repeat events; capacity mismatch | Reconcile source to qualified job; correct area/service messaging and tracking before increasing spend. |
| Contact | Unanswered/abandoned calls, no callback, after-hours silence | Solo technicians; insufficient coverage; routing or overflow failure; unowned callbacks | Inspect representative logs and coverage; assign callback owner, correct routing, evaluate staffing/overflow. |
| Speed | Slow web replies, delayed callbacks or quotes | Shared inbox with no owner; competing duties; quote bottleneck | Timestamp actual contact; assign queue and feasible service standard, escalate exceptions. |
| Follow-up | One attempt, neglected unsold estimates/inquiries | No next-action date or owner; poor visibility; unresolved objections | Review eligible open leads; create task queue, owner and appropriate contact cadence. Stop on opt-out/decline. |
| Booking | Scheduling friction, delays, cancellations, no-shows | Unsuitable slots, unclear fees/arrival windows, rescheduling barriers | Review cancellation reasons; clarify expectations, accessible rescheduling and reminders where useful. |
| Closing | Estimates not completed, quotes delayed, estimates unsold | Capacity, price/value mismatch, unclear scope, customer timing, financing or communication friction | Separate unfinished quotes from rejected quotes; review reasons, scope clarity and follow-up. Do not infer price is always the issue. |
| Retention / Existing Customer Opportunity | Lapsed maintenance, recurring churn, absent referral/review/past-customer follow-up | No appropriate due-date or owner, service experience issues | Assess relevant eligible customers; improve experience and scheduled follow-up. Respect preferences; keep separate from new-lead funnel. |

Trade adaptation examples: HVAC/plumbing can mix emergency service and installation; roofing/remodeling have longer quote-to-decision cycles; electrical may have direct service bookings and projects; landscaping/cleaning/pest control may prioritize recurring work; garage doors often combine urgent repair and replacement. Ask about the actual model rather than assigning stages solely by trade name.

For each candidate solution compare a simple process correction, staffing/schedule/routing option, conventional software automation, and AI only when the task benefits from flexible language interpretation. Automation needs an owner, input quality, exception handling and a way to verify outcomes. High volume alone does not prove AI is required.
