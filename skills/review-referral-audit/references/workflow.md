# Review & Referral Audit: diagnosis

Journey: completed job → service-quality follow-through → neutral feedback/review invitation → optional referral → suitable referred inquiry → completed work.

Verify completed work and appropriate timing. Distinguish internal feedback, public review invitation, a posted review and a new referred inquiry; these are separate outcomes. Do not fabricate reviews, testimonials, customer consent or posted responses. Review invitations must be neutral, with no requirement for a positive rating, suppression of negative feedback or incentive tied to positive sentiment. Do not filter public review requests to only happy customers. Address unresolved service issues without making resolution conditional on editing a review.

Separate requesting a referral from receiving a consenting introduction. Do not collect or contact a third party from an unsupported name/number alone. Check owner, channel/preferences, repeat-request suppression, service completion trigger and human escalation. A low review count does not prove poor service or lost revenue. For platform-specific rules or tactics, verify current official platform guidance; do not promise ranking gains.

Separate symptom, cause hypothesis, alternative explanation and validation step. Labels and limitations belong with findings. Use anonymized examples/aggregates where possible; no credentials or unnecessary customer information. A brief focused report is preferable to repeating unrelated funnel questions.
