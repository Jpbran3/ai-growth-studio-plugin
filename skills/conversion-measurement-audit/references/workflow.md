# Conversion Measurement Audit: diagnosis

Journey: source records → stable opportunity identity → comparable stage/cohort → reconciled outcomes → reliable reporting.

Start with the decision the owner wants to make, then identify minimal source exports/definitions. Call attempts, form submissions, customers, unique opportunities, quote versions, appointments and jobs are different units. Create a stable anonymized opportunity identity where possible; uncertain matches remain unresolved, not silently joined. Separate event counts from latest-state counts; preserve revisions and reschedule history without inflating stage totals.

Define received/created versus outcome dates, observation cutoff, timezone and normal pending stages. This-month jobs from older leads cannot be divided by this-month new inquiries as cohort conversion. Distinguish confirmed appointment, attended visit, accepted quote, completed job and paid invoice. Refunds/rework/revenue adjustments need explicit definitions. Public website analytics do not prove phone/CRM conversions. Attribution can be missing/multiple and is not causality. Avoid unnecessary collection of customer identifiers.

Separate symptom, cause hypothesis, alternative explanation and validation step. Labels and limitations belong with findings. Use anonymized examples/aggregates where possible; no credentials or unnecessary customer information. A brief focused report is preferable to repeating unrelated funnel questions.
