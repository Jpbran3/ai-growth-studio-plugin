# Practical process design

Create a selection rule and exclusions, suggested list owner, modest owner-approved pilot, offer grounded in actual services, reviewed channel/preferences, human handling of replies, scheduling check and stop conditions. Draft a helpful invitation without fabricated urgency, discounts, opt-in or technical claims. Separate pilot planning from sending: no list upload, CRM edits, campaign enrollment or customer contact occurs through this skill. Verify applicable platform requirements when a platform-specific execution plan is requested rather than claiming universal messaging rules.

Suggested assignments, timing and targets are proposals until the owner agrees. Give a manual alternative and failure path for every automation recommendation. Confirm inputs, accountable role and exception handling before any later implementation.
