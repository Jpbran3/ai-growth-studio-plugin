# Oak Electrical
## Revenue Leak Audit

Preliminary audit based on the owner's account. Date: October 2, 2026. Period and counts unknown. No further answers required for this preliminary result.

### 1. Executive Summary

The owner reports that the office closes at five and missed calls have no assigned callback owner. This establishes a process ownership gap in the account, with MEDIUM confidence. It does not establish how many customers or jobs are lost. Start by assigning callback responsibility and checking what actually happens when the office is closed.

### 2. Current Lead-to-Job Funnel

New inquiry → answered call or voicemail → callback/contact → relevant service booking or estimate → completed visit → accepted job. This is a provisional journey to validate; not all electrical work needs a separate estimate. Counts, periods, booking rates and job values are UNKNOWN.

### 3. Revenue Leak Map

Contact: owner-reported lack of missed-call ownership. MEDIUM confidence in the reported process gap; LOW confidence in revenue effect. HIGH priority for inexpensive validation and assignment. After-hours loss is suspected because office hours alone do not prove what routing or coverage exists.

### 4. Top Opportunities

No supported dollar estimate is available. The useful operational opportunity is ensuring every eligible missed inquiry gets an assigned next action. Call counts, unique suitable prospects, later recovery, conversion and deliverable capacity would be needed to model potential revenue.

### 5. What Should Be Fixed

Assign a person or role to review unanswered inquiries and own callbacks. Verify closed-hours routing and voicemail expectations before changing the system. Do not infer that callers are ignored simply because the office closes.

### 6. Quick Wins

Suggested office lead or owner: create a simple list with inquiry, time received, next action and responsible person; check it during feasible work windows. Test the business phone from another line during closed hours without making customer calls or changing systems automatically.

### 7. Automation Opportunities

Not warranted by current evidence. If manual tasks remain unowned after responsibilities are clear, conventional task reminders may help. Any future automation needs a responsible person and failed-notification fallback. AI is not required by this finding.

### 8. Larger System Improvements

Use one inquiry record and record actual contact outcomes. Separate booked service calls from project estimates. Confirm coverage and job capacity before buying more leads or tools.

Source: owner account, not inspected logs. Business hours and callback ownership are owner-reported. Funnel stages are provisional. No website was supplied or inspected.
