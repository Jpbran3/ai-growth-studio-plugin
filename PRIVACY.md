# AI Growth Studio Plugin — Privacy Notice

Updated October 2, 2026. Applies to development version 0.2.1 of this skills-only plugin.

## What this plugin does

The plugin supplies instructions, report templates, and a local calculation helper for auditing home service lead handling. It has no AI Growth Studio-hosted backend, analytics tracker, account system, or live connection to CRM, phone, advertising, or scheduling systems.

## Information you provide

You may supply business details, operational figures, a public website, or files to the AI host running the plugin. The host processes that information under its own terms, privacy policies, settings, and permissions. Installing this plugin does not automatically send your audit inputs or generated reports to AI Growth Studio.

The skill uses the conversation as its working evidence ledger and creates report files when requested. Those files remain in the environment where the host creates them unless you choose to share or publish them. This plugin cannot determine or override the host's conversation retention, security, or account settings.

## Websites and tools

If you supply a public website, the AI host may inspect it with an available browsing tool. The calculation helper performs local arithmetic and makes no network calls. Other host tools operate under the host's permissions and policies.

## Your choices

Use aggregated or anonymized figures when possible. Credentials and personal customer details are unnecessary for this audit. You can decline promotional content, request a preliminary report, or stop the audit. Use your AI host's controls to manage conversations and generated files.

If you separately contact AI Growth Studio for implementation support, that communication is outside the automatic behavior of this plugin. This notice does not describe a future integration or service that has not been implemented.

## Questions and changes

For questions about the plugin's behavior, open an issue at https://github.com/Jpbran3/ai-growth-studio-plugin/issues. Issues in this public repository are public; do not post customer details or credentials. For account data requests, use your AI host's support and privacy controls.

Update this notice before adding any hosted data collection, external integrations, or new data flows.
